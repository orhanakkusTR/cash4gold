Live spot price ticker

The strip at the top of this page updates continuously from the live market and is designed to sit at the very top of every page on the site — a constant signal that our offers track real-time prices.

How it works
• Pulls live Gold / Silver / Platinum / Palladium spot from the same price engine as the estimator.
• Re-polls every 60 seconds; ▲ / ▼ shows the move since the last poll.
• Toggle oz / gram on the right.
• Hover to pause the scroll. Respects reduced-motion settings.
• If the live feed drops, it falls back to reference prices and says so — never shows a stale price as “live”.

To embed: copy the block between the EMBEDDABLE TICKER START/END comments (plus its <script>) into the site header. In the Next.js build this becomes a <LivePriceTicker /> client component.
