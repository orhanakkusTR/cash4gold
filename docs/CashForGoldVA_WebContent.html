How It Works

No Appointment. No Waiting. No Guessing. Just Get Paid.

No pressure. No surprises. No charge if you walk away.

1
Bring In Whatever You Have
Don't sort it, don't research it, don't worry about it. Bring in your jewelry, coins, watches, flatware — even the stuff you're not sure about. A tangled chain, an old dental crown, a jar of coins, a broken bracelet. We've seen it all. Come as you are.
2
We Sort & Group Everything for You
We go through every item with you, one by one. Gold gets grouped by karat, coins by type, watches evaluated separately. If we don't buy something, we tell you exactly what it is and how you might get value from it elsewhere. No guesswork left on the table.
3
Every Item Is Tested on the Spot
Each piece goes through our XRF gold analyzer — the same technology used by refineries. It identifies the exact metal content down to the precise karat or purity. You watch every test happen, right in front of you, on camera.
4
We Calculate Your Offer on Our Platform
We enter every item into our own system, which calculates your payout using the live spot price — gold, silver, platinum, or palladium. You see every line item, every number, every calculation. Nothing is hidden, nothing is estimated.
5
You Review & Sign
We print out a full itemized receipt — every item listed, every price shown. You review it, you sign it. No surprises.
6
You Get Paid — Before You Leave
We issue your payment by check, right then and there. We work with a local bank, so you can cash it the same day if you'd like. No waiting, no wire transfers, no "we'll mail it to you."
💬
And if you decide not to sell? No charge. No problem. We don't pressure anyone. We're confident enough in our offers that we don't need to. Take the evaluation, shop it around, come back if you want. We'll be here.
