# Cash for Gold VA — Site & System Audit
**Date:** 2026-07-15 · **Scope:** Full SEO + System (baseline) ·
**Commit:** 34f7377 · **Pages audited:** 99 indexable (7 static, 25 category+subcategory, 4 locations, 21 city landings, 21 blog, 21 coin item) + 5 noindexed

## Executive summary

The site is well-engineered at the foundation — clean canonicals, a disciplined 110-rule redirect map, honest noindexing, strong SSG/image performance, and genuinely rich city-landing content. The problems are concentrated in three places: (1) **one confirmed conversion-breaking bug** — the phone CTA on every coin listing page is a broken relative link that 404s and is never tracked; (2) a **thin/scraped programmatic surface** — 21 coin item pages carry ~50 unique words plus competitor-scraped (JMBullion) imagery and branding tokens; and (3) **weak E-E-A-T and measurement trust** — no named appraisers, an inaccurate privacy policy, self-serving review schema, and an open/un-filtered tracking endpoint. The single highest-leverage fix is the `tel:` bug (one line, restores the primary money action on the coin matrix); a close second is the homepage shipping "0 Years in business / 0 Locations" to crawlers (P0-2). This is the baseline run; future audits track deltas against it.

## P0 — Fix now (shipping broken/false signals today)

### P0-1 · Phone CTA on all coin listing pages is a broken relative link (404 + untracked)
- **Dimension:** Technical SEO / Code health / Analytics
- **Evidence:** `src/components/coin-listing-grid.tsx:83` — `href={PRIMARY_PHONE_HREF}`. `PRIMARY_PHONE_HREF` = `"+15712245279"` (bare, no `tel:` — `src/data/business.ts:105,166`). Every other component wraps it (`cards.tsx:58,98`, `cash-banner.tsx:91`, `city-landing.tsx:149,292` all use `` href={`tel:${…}`} ``). **[VERIFIED]**
- **Source:** code-inspection
- **Impact:** The browser resolves `+15712245279` as a **relative URL** → `/coins/sell-gold-coins/+15712245279` → **404** (`dynamicParams=false`). Three harms on the coin matrix: (1) the primary call action does nothing / lands on a 404 — lost revenue for a call-driven business; (2) thousands of crawlable broken hrefs re-pollute the GSC 404 report just cleaned in commit 34f7377; (3) `analytics.tsx:79` only counts clicks whose href starts with `tel:`, so **zero** phone conversions ever fire from these pages — the loss is invisible in your own dashboard.
- **Fix:** `src/components/coin-listing-grid.tsx:83` → `` href={`tel:${PRIMARY_PHONE_HREF}`} ``.
- **Effort:** S

### P0-2 · Homepage ships "0 Locations" and "0+ Years in business" to crawlers (NumberTicker SSRs 0)
- **Dimension:** E-E-A-T / Core Web Vitals / Content
- **Evidence:** `src/components/ui/number-ticker.tsx:71` renders `{startValue}` (default `0`); the real value is written to `textContent` only client-side after hydration + in-view (`:33-58`). Used once, on the homepage trust stats: `src/components/trust-stats.tsx:51` with `value={4}` (Locations) and `value={15}` (Years). **[VERIFIED against live rendered HTML — Googlebot UA]:** `<span class="…text-gold-gradient">0</span>` for Locations and `0</span><span…>+</span>` for Years.
- **Source:** rendered-output
- **Impact:** Google, AI crawlers, and any no-JS render see **"0 Locations"** and **"0+ Years in business"** on the single most important page of a trust-dependent local business — the exact opposite of the intended signal. "0 years in business" actively harms E-E-A-T/trust and the entity's credibility on the homepage. (The hero's separate hardcoded "15 years in business" text and the plain-text "500+ / 4.9★" render fine — only the animated stat block is affected.)
- **Fix:** SSR the real value: change `number-ticker.tsx:71` to render the formatted `value` (crawlers/no-JS see the truth; the spring handler overwrites `textContent` on hydration so the count-up still works). Or pass `startValue={value}` where SSR correctness matters. Add a rendered-HTML assertion to the deploy smoke test so this class of bug can't reship.
- **Effort:** S

> **Audit-integrity note:** P0-2 was missed in this run's first pass — the rendered-output check saw the hero's hardcoded "15 years" and the plain-text review counts and wrongly cleared the ticker. Caught on review of `number-ticker.tsx:71`. The "no 0 bug" claim elsewhere in an earlier draft of this report was wrong and has been corrected. Lesson folded into method: grep every `NumberTicker`/count-up usage and diff its SSR output, don't spot-check by eye.

### P1-2 · Breadcrumb hardcodes "Gold Coins" on all item pages — wrong on the 11 silver pages (visible + JSON-LD)
- **Dimension:** Technical SEO / Metadata / Content · **Evidence:** `src/app/[category]/[sub]/[item]/page.tsx:425` and `:433` — `{ name: "Gold Coins", url: … }` unconditional; `/coins/sell-silver-coins/american-silver-eagle` emits "Home › Gold Coins › …" in both the visible crumb and `BreadcrumbList` schema. **[VERIFIED — flagged by 4 lanes]** · **Source:** code-inspection
- **Impact:** Factually wrong SERP breadcrumb + wrong topical signal on 11 of 21 pages, on the exact pages built to win "sell silver coins." · **Fix:** Derive the crumb from data — `getSubcategory(category, sub)?.name` — in both `BreadcrumbJsonLd` and `PageHero` crumbs. · **Effort:** S

### P1-3 · Self-serving, duplicated AggregateRating schema (Google-sourced) across 5 entities
- **Dimension:** E-E-A-T / Local SEO · **Evidence:** `src/components/json-ld.tsx:33-36` (Organization) + `:82-87` (each of 4 JewelryStore nodes) emit identical `aggregateRating {4.9, 500}` from `business.ts:26`; code comment at `:15-22` shows a prior "multiple aggregateRatings" GSC error. **[VERIFIED]** · **Source:** code-inspection
- **Impact:** Google's review-snippet policy disallows self-serving Organization-level ratings and ratings aggregated from third-party platforms (these are the Google/GBP numbers); each store also claims the same 500 count (no store has 500). Worst case: structured-data manual flag on a money site. `count:500` is a static hardcode that will silently go stale. · **Fix:** Remove `aggregateRating` from `OrganizationJsonLd`; on JewelryStore, either remove it or populate per-store from real GBP numbers. Keep the visible 4.9★ badges (legitimate display). · **Effort:** S

### P1-4 · Privacy policy materially misdescribes data collection (VCDPA-relevant)
- **Dimension:** E-E-A-T (Trust) · **Evidence:** `src/app/privacy/page.tsx:24-26` claims data is used "solely to respond… do not sell or share… for marketing," while the site runs OpenAI Ads Pixel + phone-conversion events, Google Ads matching, first-party click tracking to Postgres (`/api/track`), and an Elfsight third-party script. · **Source:** code-inspection
- **Impact:** An inaccurate privacy policy on a YMYL money site is a direct Trust failure and a legal exposure under Virginia's CDPA. · **Fix:** Rewrite `/privacy` to disclose advertising pixels/conversion tracking (name partners), analytics, third-party embeds, cookies, retention, and a data-request contact. **[Legal review recommended.]** · **Effort:** M

### P1-5 · 21 coin item pages: thin unique content + scraped competitor imagery/branding

### P1-10 · Analytics: /api/track is an open write endpoint (no bot filter, dedupe, or rate limit)
