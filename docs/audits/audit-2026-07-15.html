Phone CTA on all coin listing pages is a broken relative link (404 + untracked)

Evidence: coin-listing-grid.tsx:83 — href={PRIMARY_PHONE_HREF} where the value is bare "+15712245279" (no tel:). Every other component wraps it.
Impact: Browser resolves it as a relative URL → /coins/…/+15712245279 → 404. Primary call action dead on the coin matrix, re-pollutes the GSC 404 report, and no phone conversion ever fires (tracking only counts tel: hrefs).
Fix: href={`tel:${PRIMARY_PHONE_HREF}`} · Effort: S
E-E-A-T · Core Web Vitals · Content · VERIFIED (rendered-output)

Homepage ships “0 Locations” and “0+ Years in business” to crawlers

Evidence: ui/number-ticker.tsx:71 SSRs {startValue} (=0); real value written client-side only after hydration. Used at trust-stats.tsx:51 (values 4 & 15). Live Googlebot-UA HTML shows <span…>0</span> for both stats.
Impact: Google / AI crawlers / no-JS see “0 Years in business” & “0 Locations” on the most important page of a trust-driven business — the opposite of the intended signal, a direct E-E-A-T hit. (Hero’s hardcoded “15 years” & “500+/4.9★” text render fine — only the animated stat block.)
Fix: SSR the real value in number-ticker (spring still animates on hydration); add a rendered-HTML deploy assertion · Effort: S
